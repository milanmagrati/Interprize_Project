What each role can do

  • Owner Everything, plus staff accounts and invites.
  • Admin Everything except staff accounts — including the Staffs list, staff types, cities and coupons.
  • Editor Content and events, so they can put staff on an event's crew but not add one.
  • Viewer Reads everything, changes nothing.

A role is about the panel. What somebody actually does at an event is their staff type, over on the Staffs page.
